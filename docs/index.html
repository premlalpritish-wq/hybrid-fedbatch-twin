Section 02 : Problem Motivation & Mathematical Design

Industrial Inspiration: Bridging Mechanistic Rigor & ML Residuals

Inspired by biopharma upstream process development specifications (Eli Lilly's AI/ML Upstream Cell Culture Modeling & Process Development benchmark, R-108742) targeting predictive digital twins that survive benchtop-to-manufacturing scale transfer under ICH Q8-Q14 expectations.

Why Black-Box & Parallel Neural ODEs Fail CMC Review

In fed-batch recombinant E. coli BL21 fermentation, simple Monod + Luedeking-Piret equations capture stoichiometry well at low cell density, but drift severely once high cell density alters broth viscosity (damping oxygen transfer k_L a), Crabtree overflow triggers inhibitory acetate synthesis, or impeller shear tip speed and starvation accelerate unmeasured cell lysis (X_d).

Adding an unconstrained neural residual outside the balances (dx/dt = f_mech + f_nn) breaks mass conservation, can push states negative (S < 0), and fails across reactor volumes (5 L to 1000 L). Following von Stosch et al. (2014) and Schweidtmann et al. (2024), our twin enforces a strictly serial grey-box structure where GAM-86 predicts only intensive biological and rheological multipliers (delta_kd, eta_kla, q_A_prod, q_A_cons, eta_mu) inside the invariant stoichiometric shell.

dX_v/dt = (mu_mech * eta_mu - (k_d0 + delta_kd) - D) * X_v
dX_d/dt = (k_d0 + delta_kd) * X_v - D * X_d
dS/dt = D*(S_feed - S) - (mu/Y_XS + m_s*s_av + q_P/Y_PS + q_A_prod/Y_AS)*X_v
dC_L/dt = k_La_nom*eta_kla*(C* - C_L) - 1000*(mu/Y_XO + m_o*o2_av + q_A_cons/Y_AO)*X_v - D*C_L

Mass-Balance Positivity & Unclipped Stiff Oxygen EKF

Oxygen transfer at high RPM creates a stiff eigenvalue around lambda * dt = -149.95 over a 0.1 h control interval. Iteration 0 clipped Jacobian eigenvalues artificially (distorting covariance) and allowed Euler steps to drive substrate S negative at starvation boundaries.

phi_1(z) = (1 - exp(-z)) / z (with smooth Taylor series for |z| <= 1e-5)
C_L(k+1) = C_L(k) + dt * phi_1((k_La + D + dOUR/dC_L)*dt) * (k_La*(C* - C_L) - OUR - D*C_L)
EKF Covariance: P_k+1|k = expm(F_k * dt) * P_k * expm(F_k * dt)^T + (Q_base + G * Sigma_NN * G^T) * dt

Paired with the 7x5 stoichiometric Jacobian G projecting epistemic standard deviations [std_kd, std_kla, std_qp, std_qc, std_mu] directly into state covariance and Joseph-stabilized updates, both plant and filter stay strictly non-negative while cutting closed-loop state nRMSE by more than 50% compared to the Nominal EKF across all scenarios.
