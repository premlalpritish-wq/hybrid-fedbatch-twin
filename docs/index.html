02 / Problem Motivation & Mathematical Design

Serial Grey-Box Physics and Geometric Scale Transfer

Inspired by biopharma upstream process development specifications (Eli Lilly R-108742 Upstream Cell Culture Modeling benchmark) targeting predictive digital twins that preserve conservation across 5 L to 1000 L vessels under ICH Q8 to Q14 expectations.

Why Black-Box and Parallel Neural ODEs Fail CMC Review

In fed-batch recombinant E. coli BL21 fermentation, Monod and Luedeking-Piret equations capture stoichiometry at low cell density, then drift once high biomass viscosity damps oxygen transfer (k_La), Crabtree overflow triggers inhibitory acetate accumulation, or impeller shear tip speed and starvation accelerate unmeasured cell lysis (X_d).

Adding an unconstrained neural residual outside the balances (dx/dt = f_mech + f_nn) violates mass conservation, can push substrate or dissolved oxygen negative, and breaks across reactor scales. Following von Stosch et al. (2014) and Schweidtmann et al. (2024), this twin enforces a strictly serial grey-box structure where GAM-86 predicts only intensive biological and rheological rates inside an invariant stoichiometric shell.

dX_v/dt = (mu_mech * eta_mu - (k_d0 + delta_kd) - D) * X_v
dX_d/dt = (k_d0 + delta_kd) * X_v - D * X_d
dS/dt = D*(S_feed - S) - (mu/Y_XS + m_s*s_av + q_P/Y_PS + q_A_prod/Y_AS)*X_v
dC_L/dt = k_La_nom*eta_kla*(C* - C_L) - 1000*(mu/Y_XO + m_o*o2_av + q_A_cons/Y_AO)*X_v - D*C_L

Reference hydrodynamic baselines match equal gassed power per volume (Pg/V) and superficial gas velocity (v_s): (450 RPM, 1.20 vvm) at 5 L maps to (134.8 RPM, 0.205 vvm) at 1000 L with k_La_nom invariant within 1e-6 relative error.

Mass-Balance Positivity and Unclipped Stiff Oxygen EKF

Oxygen transfer at high RPM creates a stiff eigenvalue around lambda * dt = -149.95 over a 0.1 h control step. Iteration 0 artificially clipped Jacobian eigenvalues (distorting covariance) and permitted Euler steps to drive substrate S negative at starvation boundaries.

phi_1(z) = (1 - exp(-z)) / z (smooth Taylor expansion for |z| <= 1e-5)
C_L(k+1) = C_L(k) + dt * phi_1((k_La + D + dOUR/dC_L)*dt) * (k_La*(C* - C_L) - OUR - D*C_L)
EKF Covariance: P_k+1|k = expm(F_k * dt) * P_k * expm(F_k * dt)^T + (Q_base + G * Sigma_NN * G^T) * dt

Combined with the 7x5 stoichiometric Jacobian G projecting epistemic standard deviations [std_kd, std_kla, std_qp, std_qc, std_mu] into state covariance and Joseph-stabilized updates, both plant and filter stay strictly non-negative while cutting closed-loop nRMSE by more than 50% compared to the Nominal EKF across all scenarios.
