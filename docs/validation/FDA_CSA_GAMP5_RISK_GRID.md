# FDA CSA & GAMP 5 Risk-Based Assurance Grid

**System:** `hybrid-fedbatch-twin`  
**System Classification:** Portela Level 1 Offline Digital Model / Level 2-Ready Soft-Sensor Architecture (*in-silico* simulation harness, FastAPI/SQLite service, read-only/draft-staging MCP server, and offline MLOps/CAPA governance stack).  
**GAMP 5 Software Category Mapping:** Category 4 (Configured / Parameterized Standard Packages: FastAPI, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy, SciPy/NumPy, Streamlit) + Category 5 (Custom Scientific Physics, Hybrid Neural ODE `GAM-86`, EKF, OT/LIMS Adapters, Guardrails, and Part 11 Decision Ledger).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Per FDA Computer Software Assurance (CSA) and ISPE GAMP 5 Second Edition principles, assurance rigor scales proportionally with **Patient Safety / Product Quality / Data Integrity impact**. High-risk components require scripted adversarial, numerical, boundary, and tamper-detection testing; medium-risk components rely on scripted contract/integration checks; open wet-lab/GMP facility integrations not present in the codebase are isolated in [`OPEN_ASSUMPTIONS_AND_GAPS.md`](OPEN_ASSUMPTIONS_AND_GAPS.md).
